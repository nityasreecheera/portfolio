Other Events

When something important happens at a US public company, it files a form called an 8-K. Most sections of the form have a fixed topic, like a new CEO or a signed deal. Item 8.01, “Other Events”, is the catch all: anything the company wants investors to know that doesn't fit anywhere else. I used Claude to read 24,091 of these sections and asked a simple question: is this news, or a repeat?

Most of it is new news

It's easy to assume Item 8.01 just repeats the rest of the filing. It doesn't. In 77% of filings, Item 8.01 reports something that no other section of the same 8-K mentions. Only 23% repeat another section.

If you read only the main sections of an 8-K, you miss most of what Item 8.01 says. Analysts, investors and anyone building tools on SEC filings should read it too.

What the new news is about

  • Money going in and out. Dividends, borrowing and share sales are the three most common kinds of news, in about 40% of filings.
  • Some news shows up only here. Dividends (96% new), buybacks (93%) and lawsuits (92%) almost never appear in another section. For these, Item 8.01 is the only place to find them.
  • The repeats have a pattern. Leadership changes (43%) and share sales (41%) repeat the most. Usually the same press release is filed twice, or the signed agreement behind a share sale also goes in its own section.
  • Drug companies repeat the most. A third of life sciences filings are repeats, mostly because biotechs often file the same trial results press release in two places.

How I did it

  • Collected the filings. Every 8-K with an Item 8.01 from SEC EDGAR, January 2024 to January 2026, for public companies with a stock ticker.
  • Labeled the news. Claude read each Item 8.01 and tagged it with one or more of 27 kinds of news, a more detailed type within each, and a tone. On 40 test filings, Claude Sonnet got about 98% right against 90% for the cheaper Haiku, so I used Sonnet.
  • Checked for repeats. I downloaded each full filing, split it into its numbered sections, and had Claude compare Item 8.01 with every other section: same news, related news, or different news. That was 15,948 comparisons, for about $16.
  • Fixed what the check turned up. In 55 filings the text I had collected was only a pointer like “see Item 8.01 below”, not the section itself. I pulled the real text and labeled it again.

What's next
